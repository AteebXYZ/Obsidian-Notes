Radian: **The angle subtended at the center of a circle by an arc equal in length to the circle's radius** ^radian

Centripetal Force: **The resultant force towards the center of the circle required to keep the body in uniform circular motion** ^09df88

Centripetal Acceleration: **The acceleration of an object towards the center of a circle when an object is in motion around a circle at a constant speed.** ^687664

Field of Force: **A region of space where a mass experiences a force.** ^fc649a

Gravitational Field Strength at a Point: **The force per unit mass acting on a small mass at that point** ^5f3bb0

Newton's Law of Gravitation: **The gravitational force between two point masses is proportional to the product of the masses and inversely proportional to the square of their separation.** ^add89e

Geostationary Orbit: **The satellite remains directly above the Earth's equator and moves the same direction the Earth spins with the same speed of the Earth.** ^39ed92
