$$
\begin{gather}
y = \sin x && y = \cos x && y = \tan x \\
\frac{dy}{dx} = \cos x \cdot 1 && \frac{dy}{dx} = -\sin x \cdot 1 && \frac{dy}{dx} = \sec^2 x \cdot 1
\end{gather}
$$

Examples:

$$
y=\sin 2x\\
\frac{dy}{dx}=2\cos 2x
$$

$$
y=2\tan(4x+5)\\
\frac{dy}{dx}=2\sec ^{2}(4x+5)\cdot 4\implies 8\sec ^{2}(4x+5)
$$
>[!important]
>PBA Rule:
>- P: Power
>- B: Bracket
>- A: Angle

Example:
$$
\sin ^{4}2x\\
y=(\sin 2x)^{4} \quad {\color{teal}\text{Step 1 Power}}\\
\frac{dy}{dx}= 4(\sin 2x)^{3}(\cos 2x)(2)\quad {\color{teal}\text{Step 2 and 3, dy/dx of bracket \& angle}}\\
\\
{\color{cyan}8\sin ^{3}2x\cos 2x}
$$


Product rule example:
$$
\begin{gather}
&y=e^{-2x}\sin 3x\\
&u=e^{-2x}  \quad v=\sin 3x\quad u'=-2e^{-2x}\quad v'=3\cos 3x \\
&{\color{cyan}\frac{dy}{dx}=-2e^{-2x}\sin 3x+3e^{-2x}\cos 3x}
\end{gather}
$$
